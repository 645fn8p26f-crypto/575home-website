🌸 Haiku Writer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Overview

Haiku Writer is an iPhone application designed to help users write, save, and share haiku poetry. We respect your privacy.

Information You Create

Haiku titles and poems saved within the app are stored locally on your device. The current version does not automatically upload saved poems to our servers.

Accounts

Haiku Writer does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Sharing Poems

If you choose to share a poem, Apple's iOS sharing features allow you to send it to another app or service. The recipient service may process that information according to its own privacy policy.

Analytics and Advertising

Haiku Writer does not intentionally include third-party advertising or analytics services in its current release. Apple may independently process App Store and diagnostic information according to Apple's privacy policies.

AI Features

AI assistance is not available through the current app interface. If AI functionality is introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain any associated data processing.

Data Storage and Deletion

You can delete individual saved poems within Haiku Writer. Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored data, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior.

Children's Privacy

Haiku Writer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an app-operated account or service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
